# Cover letter structure

Replace with the user's own structure if they have one.

1. Header: name and contact line, date and city, employer block, subject line "Application: <exact job title>".
2. Salutation: named person when known, otherwise "Dear <Company> Hiring Team,".
3. Opening (2 to 3 sentences): the role, where it was found, who you are in one line, and the one reason this company or team specifically.
4. Evidence paragraph 1: the strongest matching story. Situation, what you did, measurable result. Tie it to a named requirement.
5. Evidence paragraph 2: a second story covering a different requirement (collaboration, ownership, domain).
6. Gap and fit (optional, 2 to 4 sentences): name the main gap honestly and how you close it; mention study or side projects only when relevant.
7. Close: location, availability, work mode fit, thanks, interview request.
8. Sign off: "Kind regards," and full name.

Rules: every claim traces to facts.md; no generic praise of the company; no repeated CV bullet lists.
